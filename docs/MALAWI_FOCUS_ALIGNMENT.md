## 1. Purpose & Decision

The company has adopted a **Malawi-first approach to proving AI services**, with initial use cases, pilots, and thought leadership focused on Malawi while remaining open to serving any client globally. This document is the operating map for that decision: how every department stays pointed at the Malawi proof, who leads the change, what it costs, and what we commit to in the first 90 days.

Mission (v2.0): *Prove agentic AI works in Malawi by shipping real services — websites, automation, reporting, and marketing — for the organizations that need them most.*

North Star: *Malawi first. Prove it. Then the world.*

Nothing in this document changes the agent roster, registry, or departmental hierarchy (`company-registry.yaml` stays authoritative). It changes *what the existing 20 departments are for*.

## 2. The Proof Thesis

Malawi is the initial focus market. Proof is the strategy. Evidence is the moat.

1. **Lighthouse clients are living proof.** Two flagship accounts land in weeks 3–6 (one local SME, one NGO), delivered end-to-end by the agent workforce, and published as case studies with named metrics.
2. **The 5-offer portfolio is a proof engine.** Offers A→E are an escalation ladder: MWK 150K quick wins to MWK 3.5M platform licenses. Each tier wins trust for the next — no one needs to be pitched "AI" abstractly.
3. **NGO / development-partner evidence is the credibility moat.** Offer C (data, donor reporting, dashboards) targets the pain every implementing partner in Malawi has. Delivered, accurate reports are stronger than any marketing claim.
4. **Localisation is a differentiator.** Every deliverable is built for Malawi's operating conditions — low-bandwidth, mobile-first, MWK pricing, and mobile-money rails. No competitor in the AI services space is this embedded in the local market.
5. **The 140-agent platform is the product story (Offer E).** Each platform license proves the model is replicable beyond our human CEO — the bridge from services revenue to product revenue, and the eventual path to SADC.
6. **Public proof artifacts compound.** Every engagement yields a case study, an anonymized data artifact, or a research note, published openly. Within 12 months we hold a portfolio no fast-follower can fabricate.
7. **MWK payment rails prove commitment.** Closing a MWK 500,000 deal via Airtel Money signals we are not a foreign outfit parachuting in.
8. **Research and data sovereignty build long-term trust.** We publish research grounded in Malawian agriculture, health, and education data — positioning LightSpeed as the firm that studies Malawi, not just sells to it.

## 5. Capacity & Cost Reality (Board-Gate Requirements)

The board approved the pivot direction and required four operating facts before execution. They are recorded here.

### 5.1 Staffing

The one-human bottleneck is real. Every deliverable passes human-CEO review, so 40 paid clients in year one is not credible. First hires, restated as working targets:

| Role | When | Approx. cost (MWK/mo) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Country Operations & Delivery Lead | Month 3–6 | MWK 400–600K | Manages delivery queue, client comms, QA handoffs; removes CEO from the loop on repeatable work |
| Account Lead | Month 4–8 | MWK 250–400K | Client reception, MWK quoting, support triage |

Until hired, capacity caps the year-one plan at **20–25 paid clients**. The 12-month target (Section 3 of `MISSION_AND_VISION.md`) is set to that ceiling, not to a wish.

### 5.2 Financial snapshot (monthly, proving stage)

| Line | Est. MWK/mo | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LLM + API usage | ~250–450K | Hypothetical — must stay ≤ 10% of revenue (guardrail) |
| Hosting / infra / tools | ~150–300K | Agent runtime, dashboards, domains |
| Comms + legal + registration amortized | ~100–150K | Registrar, MRA, Data Protection filings |
| Payment processing (mobile money) | ~3–5% of collections | Built into pricing |
| Total fixed (pre-hire) | ~500–900K | Hires add MWK 650K–1M at month 3+ |

**Runway discipline:** ≥ 18 months cash at zero revenue before any scale commitment; hires triggered only when month-3 gross revenue clears MWK 5M and repeats.

### 5.3 Named NGO / development-partner targets

Pipeline credibility needs names, not aspirations. Initial targets are drawn from existing in-repo relationships and the donor data ecosystem:

- **Ministry of Agriculture + National AI Strategy stakeholders (MACRA)** — policy and government-lighthouse lane; already engaged via the Pharos and agritech initiative tracks.
- **Opportunity International Malawi** — JDA term sheet already drafted in `archive/chichewa-ai-initiative-test/`; largest near-term institutional path.
- **Zodiak Broadcasting Station** — data license + NDA drafted; media distribution partner.
- **District Health Offices / INGO program implementers (Offer C)** — the $1.66B/yr active-project donor ecosystem (DCAFS 2025) as the standing pipeline; aim is 1 named implementing partner in the first 90 days.
