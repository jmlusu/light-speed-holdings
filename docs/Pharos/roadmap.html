Thought Leadership Roadmap

24-month phased plan making the Human CEO Malawi & SADC’s leading voice on Agentic AI Company Building, Use Cases & Policy.

Vehicle: LightSpeed Holdings Principal: Jack Mlusu Horizon: 24 months
ASPIRE. ACT. ACHIEVE.

The Window

  • Malawi’s National AI Strategy and Digital Transformation Strategy are being drafted (late 2025 workshops, UNDP Inclusive Digital Transformation project, PPP Commission Digital Malawi Acceleration project) — not finalized.
  • UNESCO AI Readiness Assessment (RAM) for Malawi validated July 2026.
  • Malawi Data Protection Act (2024) in force but not AI-specific — an opening.
  • SADC Regional Workshop on Data Governance (Harare, Aug 2026) with World Bank, Smart Africa, UNESCO; SADC Digital Transformation Strategy embeds AI.
  • AU Continental AI Strategy is the reference framework; Malawi/SADC are laggards — less noise, room to be first.
  • COMESA/IDEA Malawi AI engagement (Aug 2026); ICT Minister calling for “practical implementation.”
Implication: winning category leadership here is not “become famous” — it is becoming the most-cited, most-consulted, most-referenced practitioner while the category is being defined.

The Phases

Phase 0

Foundation

Months 0–2 — go from “unstructured expert” to “documented practitioner with a public paper trail.”

  • Write the manifesto (1,500–2,000 words).
  • Make the GitHub repo a build-in-public narrative; fill the README.
  • Rebuild the personal platform (LinkedIn headline/banner/featured; site/Substack).
  • Build the institutional stakeholder map; start engaging.
  • Reactivate ICTAM and volunteer for any AI working group.
Phase 1

Visibility & Credibility

Months 2–6

  • Submit formal commentary into the National AI Strategy consultation.
  • Publish an op-ed responding to the “fragmented digital strategy” critique.
  • Publish the first quantified use case (J&S StopOver Bar).
  • Secure one speaking/panel slot (ICTAM, COMESA/IDEA, Digital Malawi workshops).
  • Pitch as an expert source to ITWeb Africa, TechAfrica News, Malawi Voice.
Phase 2

Authority Building

Months 6–12

  • Make a formal, on-record National AI Strategy submission.
  • Produce a signature research artifact: “State of Agentic AI in Malawi.”
  • Speak at a regional convening (SADC, Smart Africa).
  • Pursue an advisory seat (ICTAM AI committee, Dept of E-Government working group).
  • Grow the use-case portfolio to 3–5 deployments (health, SME, financial inclusion).
  • Start the “policy translator” series (EU AI Act, US states → SADC).
Phase 3

Institutional Capture & Regional Reach

Months 12–24

  • Target a formal advisory/taskforce appointment (national or SADC working group).
  • Co-publish with a credible institution (UNESCO-ROSA, UNDP, Smart Africa).
  • Launch and own a signature event (Agentic AI Builders / SADC roundtable).
  • Expand deliberately into 2–3 other SADC states (Zambia, Zimbabwe, South Africa).
  • Secure a profile feature (not just a quote) in a regional tech publication.
