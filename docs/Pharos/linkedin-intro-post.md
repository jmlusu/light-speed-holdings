## Final Post Text

I am Jack Mlusu, and I build AI-native organizations — companies designed,
governed, and operated as human-led, agent-supported enterprises.

At **LightSpeed Holdings** we run one. Today that is 144 agents across 20
departments, each with a defined reporting chain, a five-tier human-in-the-loop
approval system, immutable audit trails, and a board that exercises genuine
oversight. We did not build this to impress. We built it to answer a question:

> How do we build organizations where AI agents perform meaningful work —
> while humans stay accountable for what matters?

Our framework — Human → Agents → Orchestration → Memory → Tools → Governance →
Value — is our attempt to make that answer legible, and to make governed,
agentic AI work in a resource-constrained African context.

We are already applying it here in Malawi:
- **SME operations** — a real, non-tech small business running agentic decision
  support for pricing, inventory, cash reconciliation and procurement.
- **Health and M&E** — automated monitoring and evaluation, supply-chain
  anomaly detection, and donor reporting.
- **Financial inclusion** — agentic workflows over mobile-money rails and
  informal savings groups (VSLA / SACCO).
- **Public services** — citizen-query agents, legislative summarization, and
  project monitoring.

The strategic window is open. Malawi's first National AI Strategy and National
Digital Transformation Strategy are being drafted now; the Data Protection Act
(2024) is in force but offers no interpretation for autonomous systems; SADC
and the African Union are building their regional frameworks in real time.

The region needs credible voices that have *built* these systems — not just
lectured about them. My mission is to help governments, corporations, and
development institutions design, govern, and deploy agentic AI across Malawi
and Southern Africa — and to build the measurement and the governance together.

If you are a policymaker, a regulator, a CIO, a development partner, or an
African enterprise leader making sense of agentic AI, let us connect.
