## Script Narrative (Full)

"LightSpeed Holdings Limited™ operates a live AI-native enterprise: 90 agents, 20 departments, five-tier human-in-the-loop governance. And we measure it the honest way. Every claim in this series maps to Input, Process, Output, Outcome. Our agent utilization reads 1.5 percent against an 80 percent target. Our build success rate reads 100 percent. We publish both, because an organization that quotes only flattering metrics is running marketing, not measurement. Where no real source exists - revenue, satisfaction, eNPS - the KPI reads n/a, never a hardcoded number. The rule: a KPI you cannot trace is a KPI you should not quote to a board. In thirty days an institution needs five numbers: a registry, a queue, utilization, success rate, dead-letter count. By ninety: cost per agent, decisions by tier, SLA adherence, and one outcome metric with a real source. Download the Malawi Agentic AI Monitor to explore how these patterns apply in-Malawi context."
