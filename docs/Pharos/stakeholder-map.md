# SADC Agentic AI Institutional Stakeholder Map

Business-facing engagement targets for the Pharos department. Maintained as the
primary network for position-building, partnership and policy influence. Each
entry notes relevance and the entry point.

## Malawi — Government & Regulators

| Institution | Relevance | Entry point |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| Department of E-Government | Leading the National AI Strategy | UNDP-run consultation workshops; written submissions |
| Ministry of Digital Transformation | Owns digital strategy | Stakeholder sessions |
| Ministry of Education, Science & Technology | Owns the UNESCO AI Readiness Assessment | Validation workshops, follow-on events |
| MACRA (Data Protection Authority) | Auth under Data Protection Act 2024 | Public consultations; technical briefing on agentic interpretation |
| PPP Commission — Digital Malawi Acceleration Project | Co-driving strategy | Stakeholder sessions |

## Malawi — Development & Multilateral

| Institution | Relevance | Entry point |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|

| UNESCO-ROSA | Regional AI ethics/readiness convener | Co-publication, workshops |

## Regional — SADC & Continental

| Institution | Relevance | Entry point |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| SADC Secretariat (Digital Economy desk) | Owns SADC Digital Transformation Strategy | Regional workshops, RISDP-linked events, advisory roles |
| COMESA / IDEA Malawi | Practical AI training/awareness | Direct engagement; Minister Namalomba's office |
| Smart Africa | Continental digital economy convener | Regional workshops (e.g. Harare Aug 2026) |

| Regional tech press (ITWeb Africa, TechAfrica News, Malawi Voice, TechCabal, Disrupt Africa) | Covering this beat | Direct pitch as an available expert source |

## Peer Country Focal Points (expansion)

Zambia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique and South Africa national AI/strategy teams — target
for SADC-level advisorship and "SADC Agentic AI Implementation Guide" briefings.

## Engagement Discipline

- Every interaction is tracked in a simple CRM.
- Start by following/engaging; do not pitch cold.
- Lead with outcomes (governed deployment), not jargon.
- Convert every engagement into a documented case study or relationship.
