## Channel Mix

| Channel | Cadence | Purpose |
|---------|---------|---------|
| LinkedIn | 2–3x / week | Primary distribution; build-in-public updates, policy takes, use-case snippets |
| Site / Substack | Monthly | Anchor pieces that LinkedIn links back to |
| Regional press op-eds | Quarterly | Credibility with institutional/government audiences |
| Speaking | Quarterly | In-person authority, network access |

**Pillar rule:** content maps 1:1 to the three pillars (Company Builder / Use
Cases / Policy). Never drift into generic "AI news commentary" — the fastest way
to blur into the noise.
