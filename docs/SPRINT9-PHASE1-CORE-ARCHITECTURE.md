## 3. USD/MWK Pricing Policy

### Policy Anchors

| Rule | Light Speed Policy |
|------|-------------------|
| **Primary price anchor** | USD for international / high-value services |
| **Local price** | MWK equivalent (indicative, re-quoted regularly) |
| **MWK validity** | 7 days from quotation date |
| **FX recalculation** | Re-quote when validity expires or scope changes |
| **Long projects** | Contract should contain FX protection clause |
| **Inflation** | Review MWK pricing quarterly |
| **SaaS** | USD-first pricing |
| **Enterprise** | USD-first pricing |
| **Local SME** | MWK-first pricing |

### FX / MWK Policy Details

- **Currency benchmarks** from source document: ~MWK 1,700, 1,740 and 2,200 per USD
- **Commercial benchmarks**, not official exchange rates
- **Qwen version recommendation**: USD anchoring with MWK treated as indicative and re-quoted regularly
- **Another version recommendation**: Dual-currency invoices and seven-day validity
- **Policy**: USD for international/high-value; MWK for local SME with 7-day validity

## 6. 135-Agent Positioning

### External Message

> "One Human CEO + AI-native workforce delivering digital transformation, automation, intelligence and AI-native company infrastructure."
>
> "The agent architecture is the production advantage, not the primary product."

### Internal vs External Positioning

| Aspect | Internal (Workforce) | External (Customer Facing) |
|--------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| **Agent count** | 135 agents as production advantage | Not marketed as product feature |
| **Positioning** | "We operate an AI-native delivery organization" | "We build and operate AI-native companies" |
| **Offering** | Systems and outcomes are the product | Customers purchase outcomes, systems, automation, IP |
| **SKU structure** | Internal operating system | Five flagship packages + tiers |
| **Value proposition** | High-value AI-enabled transformation | Business results in days/weeks not months |

### Key Principle

> **The 135 agents should not be marketed as 135 separate products.** They constitute Light Speed's internal production workforce, while customers purchase outcomes, systems, automation and IP.
