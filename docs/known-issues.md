| **Deck logo crop cache keyed by mtime** — The AI Venture Studio deck generator cached its pre-cropped logo under a fixed name and re-cut it only when the source asset was *newer* than the cache. mtime is not content identity: a replaced `brand/logo/logo-full.png` carrying an older timestamp (backup restore, artifact copy, a build step that sets timestamps) silently reused the stale crop and the deck shipped the wrong logo. Raised by the audit of PR #480 (issue #485). | The cache is content-addressed: `_cropped_logo()` keys the cached crop on a SHA-256 prefix of the source, so different content can never share an entry. Regression tests: `test_crop_cache_is_content_addressed`, `test_built_deck_uses_the_stable_picture_description`. | 2026-10-20 | 2026-10-20 | ✅ Fixed |
| **Dashboard perf gate flapped on a single slow sample** — `test_endpoint_response_time_p95[/api/v1/org-chart]` failed CI with `P95 response time 202ms exceeds 200ms` while nine of its ten samples were ~4 ms (PR #483, 2026-10-20). The gate asserted on `sorted(times)[int(len(times)*0.95)]` over 10 samples, which resolves to the **maximum**, so one outlier on a contended runner failed it; with no warm-up and a 200 ms limit ~50x the typical response, it could only trip on jitter and would not have caught a real 10x regression. Contributing factor: `_load_registry()` re-read and re-parsed the 96 KB registry on every request (8.4 ms median), and ten-plus endpoints share that path. Raised as issue #484. | The gate now measures a **median** over 20 samples (robust to one slow sample) against a **per-endpoint budget** calibrated from measured medians at 3-5x headroom, keeps a load-tolerant 2000 ms ceiling, and drops a warm-up request before sampling. The registry read is cached in `src/ai_company/dashboard/api.py`, keyed on the file's path, mtime and size, so a write (including one from another process) invalidates it immediately: `_load_registry()` 8.4 ms -> 0.26 ms median. New tests: `tests/unit/test_dashboard_registry_cache.py` (read-once, immediate invalidation, normalization, caller isolation). | 2026-10-20 | 2026-10-20 | ✅ Fixed |
