## 1. Corporate Positioning Statement

> **We build and operate AI-native companies.**

This is the corporate-level positioning. The individual offers explain *how* Light Speed does it — they do not replace the corporate narrative.

### The Architecture

```
LIGHT SPEED HOLDINGS
AI-Native Digital Transformation & Company Operating Systems
We build and operate AI-native companies.

Five capabilities: Digital → Growth → Automation → Intelligence → AI Company
One Human CEO + AI-native workforce
```

### Rationale

The strongest consensus across the competing catalog proposals is that Light Speed is **one** AI-native digital transformation company with five interconnected revenue lines — not five unrelated businesses. Customers move along a ladder: digital presence → growth → automation → intelligence → AI-native operations. The 127 agents are the **internal production workforce**, never a menu of 127 products.

## 3. External Messaging Framework

### The One Message

> **"One Human CEO + AI-native workforce delivering digital transformation, automation, intelligence and AI-native company infrastructure."**

### Supporting Message Pillars

1. **Speed** — "Business systems delivered in days or weeks—not months."
2. **Outcomes** — We sell business results, not technology novelty.
3. **AI-native** — A real operating system for companies, not a tool rental.
4. **Human accountability** — One Human CEO owns every client outcome.

### Proof Points

- Five flagship packages with fixed scope and published turnaround times
- Delivery classes from Express (≤72 hours) to Enterprise (45–120+ days)
- Recurring revenue products (Digital Care, Growth Desk, Managed AI Automation, Managed Analytics, Managed AI Workforce)
- A production workforce that scales 24/7

## 7. Positioning Do / Don't Checklist

**Do**
- [ ] Lead with the five flagship packages
- [ ] Say "we operate an AI-native delivery organization"
- [ ] Emphasize speed: days or weeks, not months
- [ ] Sell outcomes, systems, automation, IP
- [ ] Position the human CEO as accountable for every delivery
- [ ] Quote USD for international/high-value; MWK for local with 7-day validity
- [ ] Show tiered offers (Starter / Growth / Enterprise)
- [ ] Convert every project into a recurring service
- [ ] Use "AI-native company" as the corporate frame
- [ ] Present the customer journey as a ladder, not a menu

**Don't**
- [ ] Market the 127 agents as a product feature
- [ ] Present dozens of public SKUs as the primary sales experience
- [ ] Position as cheap AI labor
- [ ] Claim "AI replaces everything"
- [ ] Publish permanent MWK prices without FX re-quotation
- [ ] Lead with technical novelty over commercial result
- [ ] Let the five business lines compete against each other in front of a prospect
