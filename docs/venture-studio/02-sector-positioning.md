## 3. Competitive Shape (summary of competitive-landscape baseline)

Three market facts as of 2026-09 (see full scan for entity detail):

1. **Policy is moving fast** — AU Continental AI Strategy; SADC regional draft; Malawi National AI Strategy validated (Feb 2026); Data Protection Act 2024 in force; draft public-sector AI Bill (Aug 2026).
2. **Local supply is software-shop-shaped** — Malawi players sell custom builds + an “AI services line.” **None sell a governed agentic organization.** Category “AI company builder” is empty locally.
3. **International consultancies arrive with governance-shaped messaging at global pricing** — BCG, Deloitte Africa, Accenture; Qhala already operates in Malawi.

**LightSpeed wedge (internal baseline):** human-led, governed agentic organizations · 90-agent hierarchy (89 AI + 1 human CEO) · 20 departments · 5-tier HITL · immutable audit · RBAC · H→A→O→M→T→G→V · Malawi-first (MWK pricing, mobile money, WhatsApp-native, offline-first PWA) · CEO-as-operator.

**Honest status discipline (ADR-020 / ADR-033):** public proof stack is internal (2,373 regression tests, 90 agent configs, 5-tier gates). No fabricated client metrics.
