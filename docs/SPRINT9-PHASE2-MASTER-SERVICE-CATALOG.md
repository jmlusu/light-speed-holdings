## 7. Standard Turnaround Policy (Contract Language)

Light Speed Holdings will commence delivery upon receipt of the agreed deposit/payment and all required client inputs. Published turnaround times represent target delivery periods under normal conditions. Delays caused by client approvals, unavailable data, third-party platforms, payment providers, API limitations, regulatory requirements or scope changes will extend the delivery timeline accordingly.

### The Commercial Promise

| Customer Need | Light Speed Promise |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Digital presence | 72 hours–10 days |
| AI chatbot | 10 days |
| Business automation | 15–30 days |
| Dashboard | 10–20 days |
| Data intelligence | 30–60 days |
| AI workforce | 15–30 days to launch |
| Enterprise AI company | 60–120+ days |

**Core message**: "Business systems delivered in days or weeks—not months."
