## 1. What We Lead Out Is

**We Lead Out (WLO)** is an AI-native Salesforce consultancy founded in 2025 in
Melbourne, Australia (Beijing-adjacent delivery hub in Manila). Two founders —
Christopher Nugent and Clancy Brodrick — built a lean firm where **small
expert teams augmented by AI** replace the army-of-juniors model of traditional
consulting. Headcount: ~7–9 people. Client rating: **5.0 on AppExchange.**
Deliveries: **20+ shipped.** Median time to first value: **4 weeks.**

**Why this matters to Light Speed Holdings:** WLO is the real-world proof that
the product LSH builds (AI agent hierarchies) wins in the market on speed,
cost, and quality. It is simultaneously a **reference implementation** and a
**sales case study** for converting traditional consultancies.

## 2. The Pain It Solves (What Clients Reject)

Traditional consulting pain points, as WLO describes them:

## 4. The Internal AI Operating Model (Before / After)

WLO ran the AI experiment on itself before selling it. Their manual stack:
**OpenAI + Salesforce + n8n + Google Docs.**

| Activity | Before (manual) | After (AI-native) |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Meeting notes | Human transcription and filing | Transcript → structured solution design |
| Proposals | Hand-drafted per deal | Drafted from pre-filled prompts + company context |
| Documentation | Manual slog | AI-assisted, structured output |
| Follow-ups / QA / recaps | Manual, easy to miss | Running silently in the background |

**What the reclaimed time funded:** reusable prompt libraries and AI agents
that scale across projects, better discovery sessions, coaching clients
through adoption with working examples — not theory.

## 6. Entry-Point Lessons (the "Messy Middle")

From WLO's own reflections, the real traction did not come from C-suite
strategy workshops. It came from:

- Helping account executives shape enterprise deal conversations
- Supporting proposal teams to clarify half-built offers
- Co-writing and shaping value in the actual working documents
- Demonstrating real, usable "AI-first" inside the client's platform

> *"Strategy is easier to earn after you've delivered something practical,
> not before."*

**Takeaway for LSH:** lead with a tangible deliverable (an agent that
accelerates a current workflow), then upsell the platform.
