## 2. Recurring Revenue as a Core KPI

Recurring revenue is a **company-wide KPI**, not a sales nicety. Targets:

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| **Recurring revenue share** | ≥ 40% of monthly gross revenue by end of first operating quarter |
| **Project → retainer conversion** | ≥ 50% of completed projects convert to a recurring product |
| **Net revenue retention** | ≥ 90% month-over-month |
| **Churn** | ≤ 5% monthly churn on managed services |

### Why It Matters

The five business lines generate cash flow, but **recurring revenue is the engine**:

- Digital services acquire customers + immediate cash.
- Marketing creates recurring relationships.
- Automation increases customer value.
- Data/Intelligence creates premium positioning.
- AI Company Builder turns the operating system into scalable IP.
- **Managed services convert every implementation into recurring revenue.**

## 3. Project → Retainer Conversion Framework

### Conversion Triggers

| Completed Project | Natural Recurring Upsell | Trigger Point |
|-------------------|-------------------------|---------------|
| Business Website | Digital Care | At launch / handover |
| AI Chatbot Starter | Managed AI Automation | At go-live |
| AI Customer Service System | Managed AI Automation | At go-live |
| SME Workflow Automation | Managed AI Automation | At deployment |
| Donor Reporting Pack | Managed M&E | At report delivery |
| Live Executive Dashboard | Managed Analytics | At dashboard handover |
| M&E / BI Platform | Managed Analytics / Managed M&E | At platform go-live |
| AI Data Intelligence | Managed Analytics | At go-live |
| AI Workforce Setup | Managed AI Workforce | At launch |
| Campaign Sprint | Growth Desk | At campaign close |
| Landing Page Sprint | Growth Desk / SEO Program | At launch |

### Conversion Workflow

1. **Schedule conversion at kickoff** — every project is scoped with a named recurring upsell and a trigger point, agreed in the kickoff meeting.
2. **Deliver the conversion moment** — the recurring offer is presented at go-live / handover, when the system is working and the customer sees value.
3. **Offer a conversion incentive** — first month at a discount (e.g., 50% off month one) or a free onboarding month to lower the switching cost.
4. **Auto-enroll Digital Care** — every website launch includes 30 days of included support, then converts to a Digital Care retainer unless the customer opts out in writing.
5. **Track conversion** — the `sales-owner` + `cfo` agents maintain a conversion ledger: project → retainer status, trigger date, offer sent, outcome.

### The Discipline Rule

> **Every successful implementation becomes a recurring engagement unless the customer explicitly declines.** Conversion is mandatory commercial discipline, not an optional follow-up.

## 4. Qualified Enterprise Payment Terms

| Deal Size / Type | Recommended Terms |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Projects < MWK 2M** | 50% kickoff / 50% completion |
| **MWK 2M–10M** | 40% kickoff / 30% milestone / 30% deployment |
| **> MWK 10M** | 30% kickoff + milestone billing + final acceptance |
| **Monthly services** | 100% prepaid at beginning of month |
| **Annual software** | 100% upfront preferred |
| **Qualified enterprise** | Potentially 60% upfront / 40% within 90 days |
| **NGO** | PO/net terms only for vetted organizations |
| **Custom enterprise** | Contract-specific milestone/SOW |

### Qualified Enterprise Criteria (for 60/40 terms)

An organization qualifies for 60% upfront / 40% within 90 days **only** if it meets all of:

- [ ] Registered legal entity with audited financials or proven payment history
- [ ] Active purchase order or signed contract on company letterhead
- [ ] Bank transfer or corporate card as the payment rail (no mobile money)
- [ ] Contract value ≥ MWK 5M (or USD equivalent)
- [ ] Prior relationship or verifiable credit reference

**NGO / PO terms** apply only to vetted organizations with a valid purchase order and net terms agreement. Unvetted NGOs pay standard project terms (40/30/30 or milestone-based).

### FX Protection (Long Projects)

- USD for international / high-value services.
- MWK equivalent is indicative; re-quoted when validity (7 days) expires or scope changes.
- Long projects carry an FX protection clause in the contract.
- MWK pricing reviewed quarterly.
