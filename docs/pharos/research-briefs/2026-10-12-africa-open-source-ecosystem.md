## 3. Open-Source Adoption — Argument Seeds

Each seed is marked first-party (repo) or EXTERNAL. Use as scaffolding; expand only within the stated evidence.

1. **Zero marginal inference cost.** ADR-004 selects Ollama for the fast tier: "zero-cost inference", "zero marginal cost for development and testing"; live integration tests run against Ollama (free). `docs/adr/004-multi-provider-llm.md:15,92,127,130,142`. **First-party.** External cost context: DeepSeek fast-tier fallback at $0.14/1M tokens (`docs/adr/004-multi-provider-llm.md:127`).

2. **All 90 agents run locally on open-source runtimes.** "Local operates independently — all 90 agents runnable locally with Ollama/llama.cpp." `docs/ARCHITECTURE_REMOTE_RUNTIME.md:403`. Agent count independently verified: `company-registry.yaml` + `docs/source-of-truth.yaml` (counted 2026-09-23, `research/site-claims-ledger.md`). **First-party.**

3. **No per-seat licensing.** Local open-weight models mean budget-constrained deployments avoid per-seat SaaS agent licensing; free Ollama models are explicitly a budget-tier option (`docs/sop/finance-sop.md:177,187,254`; `docs/client-facing/USE-CASE-CATALOG.md:36,289`). **First-party.**

4. **Sovereignty / local models as policy alignment.** LightSpeed's formal commentary on the National AI Strategy recommends "open-weight, locally hosted models and air-gapped inference." `docs/Pharos/policy-drafts/national-ai-strategy-comments.md:52` (same pattern: `sadc-agentic-ai-governance-framework.md:56,92`). **First-party.** Aligns with Data Protection Act (2024) framing: data stays local; Ollama processes on-device with no external transmission (`docs/legal/privacy-policy.md:64,127`).

5. **Offline-first / low-bandwidth as design constraint, not blocker.** Four documented reservations (bandwidth, data protection, tech debt, AI skepticism) answered by offline-first architecture (`docs/Pharos/pillars/03-agentic-ai-resource-constrained-africa.md:19-23,64-84`; `docs/RESERVATIONS-STRATEGY.md:64,73`). Macro context: 27.7% internet penetration, 4.2% household computer ownership (`results/pestel-lightspeed-2026.md:29`). **First-party.**

6. **Skills transfer / diaspora & local dev participation (EXTERNAL).** OpenUK's *Open Source Africa Report* (27 Mar 2026) reports ~3.4M new Africa+Middle East GitHub developers (2024–25, Octoverse); Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda country data; Rwanda's open-source strategy framing "build for Rwandans, by Rwandans." https://openuk.uk/report/africa26/ — **EXTERNAL, fetched 2026-10-12.** *Caveat: country focus does not include Malawi; do not project these rates onto Malawi.*

7. **Post-donor sustainability (first-party framing, EXTERNAL macro).** Roadmap frames category leadership while strategies are being defined (`docs/Pharos/roadmap.md:21-23`); use-case catalog positions free Ollama models for "budget-constrained deployments" (`USE-CASE-CATALOG.md:289`). Sovereignty framing echoes AU strategy emphasis on open data / capacity (EXTERNAL, see §5 item 2). Do **not** claim donor funding has been secured or lost.

## 4. Gap List — Do NOT Claim

- **No partnerships.** None of the actors in §1 have a signed MoU, co-branding, or active collaboration with LightSpeed on file. "Engagement target", "co-driving strategy" (roadmap's language for UNDP/PPP), and "academic partners" (stakeholder-map's aspirational label) must not be rewritten as "partner", "works with", or "collaborates with LightSpeed."
- **No Malawi Agentic AI Forum as existing.** It is a launch goal only (§1 footnote).
- **No finalized strategy, bill, or regional framework.** Every instrument is draft/consultation/validation stage (§2).
- **No claim of endorsement by MACRA, UNESCO, UNDP, the Minister, or any listed institution.** UNESCO validated *Malawi's* RAM; that is not an endorsement of any vendor.
- **No "first", "only", "leading", "largest", or "pioneer" claims** about LightSpeed in Malawi/SADC. The repo deliberately resolved "140+ AI agents" → canonical 90 (`research/site-claims-ledger.md`); do not reintroduce superlatives.
- **No gazetted AI Bill text.** The bill's contents are unknown; only its existence and general purpose are reported (`results/pestel-lightspeed-2026.md:207`).
- **No Malawi figures from OpenUK.** Their report covers Kenya, Nigeria, Rwanda — not Malawi.
- **No "every decision auditable."** Remediated claim: "decision logs auditable on request" (`research/site-claims-ledger.md`).
- **No conference attendance or workshop participation** by LightSpeed at Harare/COMESA/UNESCO events — not evidenced.
