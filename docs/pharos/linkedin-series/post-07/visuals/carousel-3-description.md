# Post 7: Agentic AI & Financial Inclusion - Governance (G) - Carousel 3: Risk-Tiered Governance Framework

**Visual Description:** Risk-tiered governance framework showing how different risk levels trigger different governance mechanisms for financial agents.

**Key Elements:**
- Tiered risk framework with 4-5 risk levels from bottom to top
- Risk Level 1 (Lowest): Routine transactions, automated approval
- Risk Level 2: Moderate-value transactions, supervisory review
- Risk Level 3: High-value transactions, executive approval
- Risk Level 4 (Highest): Cross-border, novel products, board-level review
- Each risk tier with: risk descriptors, governance requirements, approval thresholds
- Color gradient from green (low risk) to red (high risk), with LightSpeed palette
- Decision flow arrows showing governance path per risk tier
- Statistics: percentage of transactions per risk tier, average approval times
- Title: "Risk-Tiered Governance Framework for Financial Agents"

**Purpose:** Provides a structured approach to matching governance intensity with financial risk levels in agentic AI systems.
