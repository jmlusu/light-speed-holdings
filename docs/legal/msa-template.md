**MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT**

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of {{contract_date}} ("Effective Date")
by and between:

**Light Speed Holdings**, a company incorporated under the laws of Malawi,
with its principal place of business at Lilongwe, Malawi
("Service Provider"), and

{{client_name}}, with a principal place of business at {{client_address}}
("Client").

Service Provider and Client may be referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

---

## 1. SERVICES

Service Provider will perform the professional services described in each Statement of Work ("SOW")
executed hereunder, including but not limited to: digital presence design, business process automation,
data analytics, donor reporting, and platform licensing. Services are delivered through Service Provider's
AI agent workforce ({{agent_workforce_size}} AI agents) under the direction of a human principal.

## 2. TERM

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either Party
in accordance with Section 8 (Termination), unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms herein.

## 3. FEES AND PAYMENT

### 3.1 Fees
Fees are specified in each SOW. Unless otherwise stated: 50% upfront upon SOW acceptance, 50% on delivery.

### 3.2 Currency
Fees are invoiced in **USD** for NGO/international clients and **MWK** for local Malawian SMEs,
per the Currency Policy in Exhibit A.

### 3.3 Payment Terms
- Local (MWK) invoices: due on receipt (Airtel Money/TNM Mpamba).
- International (USD) invoices: Net 30 days from invoice date. Late fee: 1.5% per month.

## 4. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

Client shall: (a) provide timely access to information and approvals as may be reasonably requested;
(b) cooperate with Service Provider's agents; and (c) designate a single point of contact.

## 5. DATA PROTECTION

### 5.1 Roles
Each SOW designates the Parties' roles (controller/processor) based on data handling.
For donor data (Offer C), Client remains **data controller**; Service Provider is **data processor**.

### 5.2 Processing
Service Provider processes Client data only on documented instructions in the applicable SOW
and the Data Processing Addendum (Exhibit B).

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 6.1 Pre-existing IP
Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property.

### 6.2 Deliverables
Upon full payment, Client receives a license to use deliverables for their internal business purpose.
Source code and templates remain Service Provider's property unless otherwise stated in the SOW.

## 7. WARRANTIES AND DISCLAIMERS

### 7.1 Services
Service Provider warrants that services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner
consistent with industry standards.

### 7.2 AI-Generated Output
DELIVERABLES ARE PRODUCED THROUGH AI AGENT WORKFLOWS. CLIENT ACKNOWLEDGES THAT ALL OUTPUT
REQUIRES HUMAN REVIEW AND CLIENT APPROVAL BEFORE RELIANCE. Service Provider does not
warrant that AI output is error-free, complete, or suitable for any particular purpose.

### 7.3 No Other Warranties
EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH ABOVE, THE SERVICE IS PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTIES
OF ANY KIND, EITHER EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION IMPLIED WARRANTIES
OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NONINFRINGEMENT.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

### 8.1 Cap
IN NO EVENT SHALL SERVICE PROVIDER'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED THE
TOTAL FEES PAID BY CLIENT HEREUNDER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

### 8.2 Exclusions
IN NO EVENT SHALL SERVICE PROVIDER BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL,
CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, DATA LOSS, OR BUSINESS
INTERRUPTION, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY THEREOF. THIS LIMITATION APPLIES
REGARDLESS OF THE FORM OF ACTION, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE),
OR STRICT LIABILITY.

### 8.3 Donor Reporting (Offer C)
For donor reporting deliverables, Client acknowledges that Service Provider's liability
is further limited to the fees paid for the specific reporting project. Service Provider
does not warrant the accuracy of underlying data provided by Client or third parties.

## 9. COMPLIANCE

Service Provider will comply with applicable data protection laws, including Malawi's
Data Protection Act 2017 and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

## 10. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 10.1 Termination for Cause
Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other Party materially breaches this
Agreement and fails to cure such breach within 30 days' written notice.

### 10.2 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, all unpaid fees become immediately due, and Client shall return or
destroy confidential information.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Malawi.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

- **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement (including all SOWs) constitutes the entire agreement.
- **Amendments:** Only via written amendment signed by both Parties.
- **Assignment:** Client may not assign without prior written consent.
- **Notices:** Via email to the designated contacts above.
