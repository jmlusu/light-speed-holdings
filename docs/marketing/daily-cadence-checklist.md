# Daily Cadence Checklist — Light Speed Holdings
**Phase:** 1+ (Post Warm-Up)
**Owner:** Marketing Owner
**Time Required:** ~2.5 hours/day team-wide

---

## DAILY (Every Weekday)

### Morning (7:00 - 9:00 CAT)

#### CEO Personal
- [ ] **7:00 AM** — Publish LinkedIn pillar post (Mon) / engagement post (Tue-Fri)
- [ ] **7:30 AM** — Publish X thread (Mon) / 5-10 replies + 2-3 original tweets (Tue-Fri)
- [ ] **8:00 AM** — 15 min commenting on peer/ICP LinkedIn posts
- [ ] **8:30 AM** — 15 min replying to X mentions + engaging large accounts

#### Company Accounts
- [ ] **8:00 AM** — Reshare CEO LinkedIn with added context (Marketing Owner)
- [ ] **8:15 AM** — Publish scheduled company post per calendar
- [ ] **8:30 AM** — Check all platforms for overnight comments → reply

---

### Midday (10:00 - 14:00 CAT)

#### Content Production (Per Weekly Schedule)
| Day | Primary Production |
|-----|-------------------|
| Mon | CEO writes next week's pillar post |
| Tue | Video shoot (Content Creator) |
| Wed | Publish hub (YouTube + Blog) + atomize |
| Thu | Atomize remaining + community |
| Fri | Analytics + next week prep |

#### Platform Posting (Per Cadence)
- [ ] **TikTok:** 3-5 videos (spaced 1-2 hrs) — Content Creator
- [ ] **Instagram:** 1 Reel + Stories — Content Creator
- [ ] **LinkedIn:** 1-2 posts (carousel/article) — Writer + Marketing
- [ ] **YouTube:** 1 Short (daily) + Long (Thu) — Creator
- [ ] **X:** 5-10 posts (replies > originals) — Marketing + CEO
- [ ] **Facebook:** 4 Reels — Marketing

---

### Afternoon (14:00 - 17:00 CAT)

#### Engagement Block (30 min protected)
- [ ] **LinkedIn:** Comment on 10 peer/ICP posts (thoughtful, not "great post")
- [ ] **X:** Reply to 20 mentions, quote-tweet 3 insights, engage 5 large accounts
- [ ] **TikTok:** Video reply to 5+ comments (prioritize questions)
- [ ] **Instagram:** DM-share 3 posts to relevant peers, reply to all comments
- [ ] **Facebook Group:** 1 discussion prompt + 5 comment replies
- [ ] **Reddit:** 3 helpful comments in target subreddits
- [ ] **Quora:** 1-2 answers (Thu/Fri)

#### Cross-Pollination
- [ ] CEO comments on company LinkedIn post
- [ ] Company reshares CEO X thread with context
- [ ] Tag partners/collaborators when relevant
- [ ] Add UTM to any new links shared

---

### End of Day (17:00 - 17:30 CAT)

#### Logging
- [ ] Record 24h metrics for yesterday's posts in tracking spreadsheet
- [ ] Note top performer + why (format, hook, timing)
- [ ] Flag any shadowban/technical issues
- [ ] Update content calendar if shifts needed

#### Prep for Tomorrow
- [ ] Review tomorrow's calendar slots
- [ ] Confirm assets ready (thumbnails, captions, UTMs)
- [ ] Slack/email team: "Tomorrow: [pillar] — [assets needed] by [time]"

---

## WEEKLY (Monday & Friday)

### Monday — Weekly Kickoff (30 min)
- [ ] Import platform CSVs → Tracking Spreadsheet
- [ ] GA4: Social traffic report (week over week)
- [ ] Identify: Top 3 formats, Top 3 topics, Bottom 3 formats
- [ ] Adjust: Next week's format mix based on data
- [ ] Sync: 15 min standup (CMO, Writer, Creator, Marketing)

### Friday — Weekly Wrap (45 min)
- [ ] Full weekly metrics dashboard update
- [ ] Content audit: Which pillar assets hit KPIs?
- [ ] Format optimization: Document learnings
- [ ] Collaboration outreach: 2 touches to potential partners
- [ ] Next week pillar brief drafted (CMO)
- [ ] Newsletter sent (if weekly) or drafted (bi-weekly)

---

## BI-WEEKLY (Every Other Friday)

### Newsletter Production
- [ ] CEO letter drafted (Thought Leadership Lead → CEO approve)
- [ ] 3 pillar highlights curated
- [ ] Community spotlight (1 follower/group member)
- [ ] Upcoming events/speaking
- [ ] Design in template → test send → schedule

---

## MONTHLY (1st Monday)

### Deep-Dive Analytics (90 min)
- [ ] **Platform ROI:** Hours invested vs. followers/traffic/leads
- [ ] **AI Citation Audit:** 20 keywords in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini
- [ ] **Backlink Health:** LynkDog report (new/lost/broken)
- [ ] **Brand Compliance:** 50 random posts audit
- [ ] **Keyword Refresh:** Add 5 new, retire 5 stale
- [ ] **Competitor Scan:** Top 3 competitors' top posts
- [ ] **Strategy Adjustment:** Document pivots for next month

### Reporting
- [ ] Executive summary (1 page) to CEO
- [ ] KPI scorecard vs. targets
- [ ] Channel breakdown with recommendations

---

## QUARTERLY (1st Monday of Quarter)

### Strategic Review (2 hours)
- [ ] 90-day KPI trend analysis
- [ ] Channel investment reallocation
- [ ] New platform evaluation (Threads, Bluesky, etc.)
- [ ] Budget proposal (tools, contractors, paid test)
- [ ] Team capacity & hiring needs
- [ ] White paper / Monitor / Speaking pipeline review

## EMERGENCY / INTERRUPTION PROTOCOL

| Trigger | Action | Owner |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Viral moment (unexpected) | Double-down: create follow-up in 2 hrs | Creator + Writer |
| Crisis/negative press | Pause scheduled → crisis comms plan | CMO + Legal |
| Platform outage | Shift to owned channels (email, blog) | Marketing |
| CEO unavailable | Thought Lead ghostwrites → async approve | Thought Lead |
| Legal blocks content | Swap to technical pillar → reschedule | CMO + Legal |
