## 2. Market Snapshot

The AI market LightSpeed operates in has three structural facts:

1. **Policy is moving fast.** African Union adopted the Continental AI Strategy (June 2024); SADC is drafting a Regional AI Strategy and Regulatory Framework; Malawi finalized validation of its National Digital Transformation Strategy and National AI Strategy (Feb 2026), passed the Data Protection Act 2024 into operation (3 June 2024), and announced a draft AI Bill for the public sector (Aug 2026).
2. **Local supply is software-shop-shaped, not org-transformation-shaped.** Nearly every Malawi player above sells custom software / web / mobile with an AI services line. None sells a governed agentic organization. The "AI company builder" category is empty locally.
3. **International consultancies are arriving with governance-shaped messaging but global pricing.** BCG, Deloitte Africa, and Accenture all sell AI strategy + governance narrative at enterprise rates; Deloitte Africa explicitly markets "GenAI and agentic AI". Qhala (Nairobi) already operates in Malawi.
