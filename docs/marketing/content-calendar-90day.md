## Weekly Waterfall Schedule (Template)

### MONDAY — CEO Pillar Post (LinkedIn + X Thread)
| Time | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 7:00 AM | CEO publishes LinkedIn pillar post (1500-2000 words) | CEO |
| 7:30 AM | CEO publishes X thread (8-10 tweets) | CEO |
| 8:00 AM | Company reshares LinkedIn with context | Marketing Owner |
| 9:00 AM | Company posts X thread summary | Marketing Owner |
| All day | 30 min commenting on peer/ICP posts | CEO + Head of DevRel |

### TUESDAY — Video Production
| Time | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| Morning | Shoot/record YouTube tutorial (10-15 min) | Content Creator |
| Afternoon | Edit + captions + chapters | Content Creator |
| Evening | Create 4-5 Shorts/Reels/TikToks from footage | Content Creator |

### WEDNESDAY — Publish Pillar Hub
| Time | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| 8:00 AM | Publish YouTube long-form + blog post (embedded) | Content Writer + Marketing Owner |
| 10:00 AM | Publish 2-3 LinkedIn carousels | Content Writer |
| 12:00 PM | Publish 3-5 TikToks (spaced 1-2 hrs) | Content Creator |
| 2:00 PM | Publish 2-3 Instagram Reels | Content Creator |
| 4:00 PM | Publish 2 Facebook Reels | Marketing Owner |
| 6:00 PM | X thread: key insights + links | Marketing Owner |

### THURSDAY — Atomize & Community
| Time | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| Morning | Quora answer (1-2) | Content Writer |
| Morning | Reddit engagement (3+ comments, 1 post) | Market Analyst |
| Afternoon | Facebook Group discussion prompt | Community Builder |
| Evening | Newsletter section draft | Thought Leadership Lead |

### FRIDAY — Engagement & Prep
| Time | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| All day | Reply to all comments (video reply on TikTok) | All |
| Morning | Analytics review (weekly) | Growth Hacker |
| Afternoon | Next week pillar planning | CMO + Content Writer |
| Evening | Community roundup post | Community Builder |

### WEEKEND — Light Touch
- CEO: Personal insights, speaking prep, reading
- Team: Optional batch creation, template updates
