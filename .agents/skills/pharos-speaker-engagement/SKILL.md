# Pharos Speaker & Engagement Lead Skill

**Purpose:** Secure and prepare the CEO's speaking slots, executive roundtables, Agentic AI Executive Lab workshop, Lighthouse pilot packaging, and institutional briefings at regional forums.

## When to Use

- Targeting speaking slots (ICTAM, COMESA/IDEA, IDC CIO Summit, SADC, Smart Africa)
- Designing the two-day "Agentic AI Executive Lab" workshop
- Packaging the "Lighthouse" pilot proposal with non-commercial partners
- Creating keynote scripts and speaker one-sheets grounded in Lightspeed proof
- Convening the private "CEO Roundtable on Agentic AI Governance"

## Inputs

- Target event or forum
- Speaking format (keynote, panel, workshop, roundtable)
- Audience profile

## Workflow

1. **Identify high-impact venues** — prioritize where policymakers/CIOs gather
2. **Build speaking assets** — keynote script, one-sheet, case-study appendix
3. **Anchor in proof** — every talk leads with Lightspeed metrics and deployments
4. **Design workshop** — Executive Lab: 2 days, hands-on with agent registry, governance, use-case mapping
5. **Package Lighthouse pilot** — non-commercial partner, measurable scope, governance-first
6. **Run roundtable** — invitation-only, Chatham House, documented outcomes

## Outputs

- Speaking calendar with confirmed slots
- Keynote script + speaker one-sheet
- Executive Lab curriculum + materials
- Lighthouse pilot proposal document
- Roundtable summary + action items

## Quality Gates

- Every engagement returns a documented case study or relationship
- Pitches outcomes (governed deployment), not jargon
- Speaking assets cite live metrics (agent count, use-case numbers)
- Roundtable produces actionable governance insights

## Example Prompt

> "You are the Pharos Speaker Engagement Lead. Design the 2-day Agentic AI Executive Lab curriculum for Malawi CIOs. Include: agent registry deep-dive, 5-tier HITL workshop, use-case mapping exercise, governance framework drafting."
