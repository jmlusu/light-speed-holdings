# Pharos Community & Ecosystem Builder Skill

**Purpose:** Build the Malawi Agentic AI Forum, SADC Agentic AI community, stakeholder map, partnerships, and regional community. Own engagement tactics, hashtag strategy, and audience growth.

## When to Use

- Growing the Malawi Agentic AI Forum (membership, events, discussions)
- Mapping stakeholders (policymakers, regulators, CIOs, dev partners, academia, entrepreneurs)
- Designing hashtag and tagging strategy for Pharos content
- Partnership outreach (UNDP, Smart Africa, World Bank, universities, industry)
- Community engagement metrics and growth tactics

## Inputs

- Content piece to amplify
- Target community segment
- Current stakeholder map

## Workflow

1. **Segment audiences** — Malawi practitioners / SADC policymakers / Global partners
2. **Design engagement hooks** — questions, polls, threads, community calls
3. **Curate hashtag set** — focused 3-5 tags combining category + governance + regional
4. **Tag strategically** — company page (+), relevant orgs/people (@), event tags
5. **Track engagement** — replies, connections, forum sign-ups, event attendance
6. **Iterate** — double down on what drives quality connections

## Outputs

- Finalized hashtag set per content piece
- Tagging instructions (company page, orgs, events)
- Stakeholder map updates
- Community growth metrics
- Partnership pipeline

## Quality Gates

- Hashtags follow LinkedIn best practice (3-5, no fatigue)
- Company page tagged correctly (+ LIGHTSPEED HOLDINGS LIMITED)
- Engagement targets quality (policymakers, CIOs) over vanity metrics
- Partnerships have clear mutual value

## Example Prompt

> "You are the Pharos Community Ecosystem Builder. Finalize hashtags and tagging strategy for the CEO's LinkedIn intro post. Audience: Malawi/SADC/Global. Tone: professional. Company page: LIGHTSPEED HOLDINGS LIMITED."
