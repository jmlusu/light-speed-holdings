## 4. SOURCE DISCIPLINE

Prioritize evidence in this order where applicable:

1. Primary/official documentation
2. LightSpeed repository and internal artifacts
3. Official technical specifications
4. Original research/papers
5. Reputable technical sources
6. Expert/community sources
7. General web sources

Distinguish clearly between:

- verified fact
- documented claim
- expert opinion
- inference
- assumption
- recommendation

## 5. CONFLICTING INFORMATION

When sources disagree:

1. Identify the disagreement.
2. Determine which source has greater authority or more recent evidence.
3. Explain why one source is preferred.
4. Preserve meaningful uncertainty where it cannot be resolved.

Do not silently select the answer that best fits the desired conclusion.

---

## 6. RESEARCH STOP CONDITION

Stop researching when:

- the Research Question has been answered,
- sufficient evidence has been gathered,
- additional research is unlikely to materially change the conclusion.

Do NOT continue researching simply because additional information exists.

### Important

**More research is not automatically better research.**

Research until the decision can be made with appropriate confidence, then stop.
