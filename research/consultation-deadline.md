# Malawi National AI Strategy Consultation — Submission Deadline & Requirements

**Research Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Prepared for:** Light Speed Holdings  
**Status:** Partial findings — portal URL and detailed submission specs not publicly documented

---

## Executive Summary

Malawi's **National Artificial Intelligence (AI) Strategy** and **National Digital Transformation Strategy** entered a public validation and consultation phase in **February 2026**. The Department of E-Government (Ministry of Information and Communications Technology), supported by UNDP's **Inclusive Digital Transformation for Malawi (IDT4M)** project, launched the **"Shape Malawi's Digital and AI Future Portal"** — a public platform to collect citizen feedback before finalising both strategies.

**Key gap:** The portal's exact URL, submission deadline, required format/templates, and late-submission policy are **not published in any accessible official source** as of this research date.
