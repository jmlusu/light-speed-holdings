short 1.61 MB, overview 1.68 MB.Evidence files: `artifacts/campaigns/.../qa/assets/assets-manifest.json` and `qa/generate-run.log`.### Two measurement decisions worth reviewing**Video palette is measured on source frames, not decoded frames.** H.264 `yuv420p` is lossy, so a decoded frame measures ~98.6% off-palette while the same rendered pixels measure 99.93% on-palette. Gating the decode would fail a correctly rendered video, so the gate reads the frames handed to the encoder; the decoded number is recorded and labelled informational. That measurement doubles as a negative control ΓÇö the palette gate demonstrably can fail.**Tolerance is 3.0%, not 0.03%.** Legitimate antialiasing puts cards 0.2ΓÇô0.3% off-palette. A genuinely off-brand asset lands in the tens of percent, so 3.0 separates the two rather than masking either.## Not in this PR`artifacts/` (campaign output ΓÇö the MP4s exceed `check-added-large-files --maxkb=500`), and the pre-existing untracked `scripts/brand_briefing.py` + `.opencode/commands/brand-briefing.md`, which belong to an earlier session and were left untouched.## Not verified, not claimed- **Open Design render path.** `OD_BIN` is unset and no daemon is running, so every asset came from the local renderer.
