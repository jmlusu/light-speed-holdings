## Original Issue Body

The S13 "Thank You" slide of the AI Venture Studio client deck rendered the
logo's baked-in tagline on top of "ASPIRE. ACT. ACHIEVE." and
"LightSpeed Holdings Limited". The structural verifier reported all checks
passing, so the defect reached the client-facing artifact.

## Root Cause (filled by the resolving engineer)

The generator placed the **full 1254x1254 square** `brand/logo/logo-full.png`
into a square 3.2817 x 3.2817 in frame and expressed the trim as a crop
declaration:

```xml
<a:off x="3000894" y="3033314"/><a:ext cx="3127248" cy="3127248"/>
<a:srcRect l="15152" r="14673" t="18501" b="24641"/>
```

- PowerPoint honours `a:srcRect` on pictures, so the slide looked correct there.
- **LibreOffice ignores `a:srcRect` on pictures.** It stretches the entire
  square bitmap into the frame, so the artwork - including the baked-in
  tagline - spills below the frame bottom (6.7369 in), landing on the tagline
  block at y 6.24 in.
- LibreOffice **does** honour `a:clrChange`, which is why the navy background
  showed through and the defect looked like "text overlap" rather than an
  opaque plate.

The structural verifier also used `a:srcRect` as the visible box, so it
measured the intended crop instead of the frame LibreOffice actually paints.

## Fix (filled by the resolving engineer)

1. Pre-crop the logo asset instead of declaring `srcRect`:
   - `LOGO_SRC_BOX = (190, 232, 1070, 945)` -> 880x713, `LOGO_ASPECT = 880/713`.
   - `_cropped_logo()` writes a cached `ai-venture-studio-lockup-880x713.png`
     to the temp dir; `_make_logo_bg_transparent()` / `a:clrChange` retained.
   - `logo()` calls `add_picture(..., width=..., height=width/LOGO_ASPECT)` with
     **no** crop, so frame and artwork geometry are identical in every renderer.
   - `LOGO_CROP` deleted.
2. Reposition S13: `<a:off x="3474720" y="3611880"/>` (3.8000, 3.9500 in),
   `<a:ext cx="2194560" cy="1778092"/>` (2.4000 x 1.94449 in), no `srcRect`;
   frame bottom 5.8945 in -> 0.3455 in clear space above the 6.24 in tagline.
3. Verifier: `pic_info()` now treats the **frame** as visible and flags any
   `srcRect` crop as non-portable; added an S13 tagline-clearance check
   (>= 0.15 in) and a picture-vs-text collision check. 9 -> 11 checks.
4. New `scripts/presentation/render-venture-studio-deck.py`: renders the deck to
   PDF via LibreOffice (`soffice.com`), rasterises the opening and closing
   slides with PyMuPDF, and asserts on real pixels (navy background, artwork
   within frame, no navy plate, tagline position, >= 0.15 in clear space).
