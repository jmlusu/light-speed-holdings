The architecture and its controls

Designed for resource-constrained (low-bandwidth/power-intermittent) environments; leverages local LLM inference (Ollama/llama.cpp) to eliminate per-token dependency and bandwidth reliance.

In our submitted commentary on Malawi's forthcoming National AI Strategy, we officially recommend open-weight, locally hosted models for sovereign data protection (aligned with the DPA 2024).

Governance

Five-tier human-in-the-loop approval system ensures auditability; Tier 2 for file edits, Tier 3 for shell execution, Tier 4 for config changes. Timeouts escalate, they never auto-approve.
