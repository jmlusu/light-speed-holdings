## Context

A weekly scan of agentic-AI and digital-governance developments across the SADC
region, feeding the monthly Malawi Agentic AI Monitor and the Fri policy
routines. This is a **deep-research** scan: prioritize corroborated, regionally
relevant items with institutional readers in mind.

## Steps

1. **Anchor on the region.** Ministry of Malawi announcements, MACRA,
   SADC secretariat publishes, AU/Digital Transformation Strategy tracks —
   plus named parks and programs already in `docs/Pharos/stakeholder-map.md`.
2. **Scan authoritative sources** (webfetch on official/primary URLs) for the
   week's agentic-AI policy, procurement, and governance moves. Prefer primary
   statements over commentary.
3. **Connect to existing context.** Recall prior scans from memory so the month
   builds a continuity narrative (avoid re-reporting last week's news as new).
4. **Flag verification status** for every item: VERIFIED (primary source seen) /
   PARTIAL (single secondary) / UNVERIFIED (mention only).
5. **Produce the scan note** — a dated markdown list, newest first, with
   per-item: development, source URL, verification status, and why it matters to
   LightSpeed's institutional positioning.

## Guardrails

- Regional freshness is the chief credibility risk: never pad with US-creator
  content, and never present commentary as primary evidence.
- At least 3 VIERIFIED/primary items each week; anything weaker is clearly
  labeled.
- Output the scan as markdown under the routine run id.
