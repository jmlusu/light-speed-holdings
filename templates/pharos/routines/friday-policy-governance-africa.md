# AI Policy & Governance Africa — Friday (UTC 04:00)

Produce the weekly Pharos AI Policy & Governance Africa brief for the Human CEO.
Voice: policy‑focused, balancing ambition with regulatory realism. Audience:
Malawi ministries, SADC secretariat, UNDP/UNESCO‑ROSA, banks, ICTAM. Cadence:
every Friday, publishing a LinkedIn long‑form + distilled email summary. Reference
docs/Pharos/roadmap.md for the current quarter theme, docs/Pharos/content-calendar.md
for this week's slot, and the Malawi Agentic AI Forum position papers for
context.

## Prompt

Write an 800‑word policy brief with the following sections, in order:
1. **Headline** — one sentence framing the week's policy focus (align with the
   quarterly theme in roadmap.md).
2. **Regulatory landscape** — 2‑3 sentences on the current AI policy environment
   in Malawi and the SADC region (cite local strategies, AU/African Union
   initiatives).
3. **Gap analysis** — 2‑3 evidence‑backed gaps in regional governance capacity
   (data availability, institutional expertise, cross‑border coordination).
4. **Recommendation** — one concrete policy recommendation for the CEO to advance
   in the coming week.
5. **Forward look** — one emerging trend to watch next week (new regulation,
   research breakthrough, regional partnership).

End with a footnote: "Data as of {date} (UTC). Sources verified via
k‑dense-research-lookup and regional verification pass."

## Prompt tokens

- `{date}` → today's UTC date (YYYY‑MM‑DD)
- `{quarter_theme}` → read from docs/Pharos/roadmap.md (if available)
