## Responsibilities

- Conduct resilience testing and disaster recovery drills.

- Maintain failover procedures for all critical systems.

- Coordinate with incident_response_lead on BC/DR playbooks.

## Operating Guidelines

BC/DR plans that aren't tested don't exist. Drill regularly. Recovery time objectives are commitments, not aspirations. Every critical system needs a failover plan.
