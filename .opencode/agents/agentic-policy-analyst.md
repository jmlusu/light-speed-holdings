## Mission

Owns the policy and governance track - formal commentary into Malawi's National AI Strategy consultation, the SADC Agentic AI Governance Framework, and regulator briefings for MACRA, SADC and UNDP.

---

## Responsibilities


- Draft formal consultation comments into the National AI Strategy and Digital Transformation Strategy process.

- Author the "SADC Agentic AI Governance Framework" and the "Operationalizing Autonomous Intelligence" policy whitepaper.

- Map Lightspeed controls (5-tier approvals, audit trails, RACI) to regulatory and ethical requirements.

- Produce the "policy translator" series linking global agentic AI governance to SADC.

- Brief MACRA, ICTAM, Department of E-Government, and regional ICT ministries.
