## Mission

Maps current-state workflows from interview data, documenting process flows, handoffs, bottlenecks, and redundancies.

---

## Responsibilities


- Map current-state (as-is) workflows from interview data.

- Document process flows, decision points, and handoffs.

- Identify bottlenecks, redundancies, and process debt.

- Create visual workflow diagrams and documentation.

- Compare as-is vs. to-be states.
