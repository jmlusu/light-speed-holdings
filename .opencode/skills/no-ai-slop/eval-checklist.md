# Eval Checklist

Run these checks after every edit. Answer each with pass or fail. If any check fails, fix the draft before returning it.

## Editing Principles

1. **Does the edit preserve the user's point without adding claims, examples, stats, quotes, or opinions?** (pass/fail)

2. **Does it preserve the writer's distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, digressions, and level of polish?** (pass/fail)

3. **Does it leave strong human sentences alone instead of rewriting them for consistency or making every paragraph equally tidy?** (pass/fail)

4. **Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual slop, with no aggressive compression that strips out character?** (pass/fail)

5. **Does the draft lead with what the reader needs while keeping personal setup that adds context, tension, or character?** (pass/fail)

6. **Are points front-loaded where that improves clarity without forcing every unit into the same structure?** (pass/fail)

7. **Do sentences earn their place, with concrete facts, protected details, and direct verbs where the draft supports them?** (pass/fail)

8. **Does every generic sentence pass the portability test, or was it cut or made specific to this subject?** (pass/fail)

9. **Does the draft use active voice with human subjects where possible?** (pass/fail)

10. **Does the edit keep useful edge and preserve structure unless the structure was hurting the piece?** (pass/fail)

11. **Are genuinely tangled sentences fixed while clear spoken cadence, fragments, and changes in pace remain intact?** (pass/fail)

## Final Read

1. **Does the draft avoid robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, and stacked punchy fragments?** (pass/fail)

2. **Would the writer recognize the edited draft as their own voice?** (pass/fail)

3. **Would the edited draft sound natural if read to a sharp colleague?** (pass/fail)

4. **Does the final output include the full edited draft and a short **What changed** section?** (pass/fail)

5. **For detect requests, does the response name each pattern with a quoted line and a short fix, without rewriting, scoring, or claiming AI authorship?** (pass/fail)
