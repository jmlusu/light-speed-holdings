## The subject

A landing page for **LightSpeed Holdings Limited**, an AI company builder whose product creates and orchestrates hierarchies of AI agents. The audience is potential clients and investors. **What the visitor does next:** stamp "Start a project". One label, used everywhere on the page (nav and close). Destination is a mailto/contact inbox.

## Step 2 - Grammar, signature move, gate, score

### Grammar: chaptered editorial

The page is a printed feature: a cover, numbered chapters with folio in the margin, plates in their own columns with captions, and a colophon close. Distinct scenes, photographic plates, calm pace. Hero is a title page. The CTA appears as a running-text line in the close.

**Why the other seven lost:**

- **Filmic one-shot**: the interview chose distinct scenes, not one unbroken audience. Also the fingerprint the first four builds all used.
- **Continuous world / worldflight**: requires one place the camera flies through; distinct scenes were chosen.
- **Rhythmic cutlist**: a pulse, hyperspeed grammar; the interview wants calm open, peak mid, resolve.
- **Live surface**: forbids full-bleed photography, wordmark-plus-CTA bars, scrims and hero claims laid over footage; the chosen world and the CEO plate need all of those.
- **Typographic poster**: forbids a photographic ground; the world here is photographic.
- **Gallery / catalog**: framed object choices; the argument here is a belief, not an options rack.
- **Split stage**: a two-column before/after comparison; it cannot hold the CEO plate or the colophon close, and reads as a pitch deck, not a feature.

**Nav / hero / close (from the grammar, not decided separately):** no fixed top bar. A folio in the margin updates chapter number and title as the reader moves. Hero is the cover/title page. Close is a colophon plate: small type, the CTA as a running text line, and the three flags standing on the horizon behind.

### Signature move: the flags we raise

Three tall flags (ASPIRE. ACT. ACHIEVE.) stand waiting on a night horizon. Scrolling raises them one at a time to full height. The third flag reaching the top is the moment the page falls still. Bespoke scroll-driven flag raising, coded in the page, not a kit parameter.

| 1 Recognition | Calm | The cover: LIGHTSPEED HOLDINGS LIMITED, the fold still, the folio waiting |
| 2 Tension | Unease | One plate of an ordinary company run by hand, copy naming the cost in the margin |
| 3 Turn | Trust | A wipe: the agent company, real figures counting in as proof |

| 6 Commitment | Resolve | The colophon: small type, "Start a project", three flags standing behind |

Six distinct emotions, none repeated adjacently. The peak is act 4, the largest span, mid-page, and the act before it is quieter.

### Peak and tell-someone sentence

**The peak** (act 4, Ambition): "three flags raised themselves over the ridge at night, and the whole page fell still once the last one was up."

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where you raise three flags over a night horizon one at a time, and the whole page goes silent the moment the third one stands.

### Authored silence

Between act 3 (the turn) and the peak: one empty viewport of horizon before the flags begin. Authored, not dead scroll.

### Scroll score (device per act)

| Beat | Device | Why this one |
|------|--------|--------------|
| 1 Recognition | `flow` + `in` | A title-page cover settles still; the folio steps the reader into chapter I |
| 2 Tension | `pin` + `parallax` | The ordinary-company plate holds while the cost is named in the margin; a slow parallax push keeps the ground alive |
| 3 Turn | `flow` (`reveal` + `count`) | A wipe is a state change; real figures (152 agents in the registry) count in as proof as the section scrolls past |
| 4 Ambition (PEAK) | `scrub` (signature) | The wheel is the only way the flags rise; largest span on the page |
| 5 Belief | `flow` + `in` | The founder's desk settles after the peak, quieter |
| 6 Commitment | `pin` (colophon close) | A short-span close; the plate resolves, CTA as a running text line, flags standing inside the stage |

Checklist:

- Bans hold: no full-bleed scrub hero (hero is a title page), no pinned crossfade type acts, no magnetic CTA, no centred hero copy, no scrub beyond this one chapter, no drift as a gradient device, no spotlight, no magnet.
- Device families: flow, in, pin, parallax, reveal, count, scrub (7).
- No device twice in a row. Scrub exactly once.
- One scrub act only (allowed up to two); flag raising is the one moment, no reprise.
- Peak is act 4, largest span, one act quieter than the peak.
- Six acts, editorial length, well short of the 13.6 to 13.8 viewport-height fingerprint band.

## Brand rules that bind the build

- Full name LIGHTSPEED HOLDINGS LIMITED; tagline ASPIRE. ACT. ACHIEVE.
- Official logo assets only; never recreate a logo.
- Palette: navy #070A40 dominant, crimson #E63946 for CTAs and the accent, cyan #00BFFF for links on dark.
