Copy the HTML below and paste it into your email client's signature settings. Update the placeholder fields with your information.

How to Install

  1. Gmail: Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new → Paste HTML

  4. Other: Look for "Signature" or "Email Signature" in your email settings
